## B1: what the netlist adds (private RE notes docs/fpga.md, 2026-11-13)

- 0x10d0 has flip-flops for bits 7:0 only; bit 15 is a dead write. The divider value itself (1 at 1.25 GSa/s, 2 at
  625 MSa/s, 0 on the second divider) still differs by rate.
- 0x1004 bit 2 reaches only the play side, the sinc chain and the trigger, not the DDR write formatter. 0x1148 drives
  play-side counters.
- In average mode the play reads every second 12-bit field: one averaged point takes two stored 12-bit fields, a
  24-bit sum (inferred from the play stub).

Two consequences, both hypotheses until the re-test:

- Zero frames at 625 MSa/s and above: averaging is a DDR read-modify-write of twice the stored width. At 625 MSa/s
  that is about 625 M x 24 bit x 2 (read and write) = 30 Gbit/s, above the DDR3 x16 interface (about 25.6 Gbit/s at
  1600 MT/s); at 312.5 MSa/s it is half that. A bandwidth limit would put the edge on the total sample rate (rate x
  interleave ratio), between 312.5 and 625 MSa/s, not at 62.5 MSa/s. Step 4 of the B1 re-test decides; if 312.5 MSa/s
  works at 1 channel and 2 x 312.5 MSa/s does not, the guard becomes `rate x ratio < 625 MSa/s`.
- Noise floor near 0.46 mV: if the play outputs the 24-bit sum shifted by log2(N) and cut back to 12 bits, every
  averaged point is a whole ADC code and the average cannot go below about one ADC LSB (16 codes on the 16-bit read
  scale, 16 / 7500 div: 0.43 mV at 200 mV/div). The software average keeps fractions. Then hardware averaging only
  pays for transfer and coverage, not for resolution. Test: B1 step 1 at 50 mV/div and at 500 mV/div. A floor that
  scales with V/div (about 16 codes) is the truncation; a fixed floor in mV is something else.

## Debug switches (system.conf, read at oos-ui start)

| key | effect |
|---|---|
| `avg.hw_fast = on` | hardware averaging above 62.5 MSa/s too |
| `avg.debug_sinc = on` | 0x1004 bit 2 set after every configuration in average mode (`acq::force_avg_sinc`) |
| `avg.debug_raw = seq` | after each of the first 4 batches after a clear: the newest frame played again with 0x1004 bit 3 cleared, twice the points from twice the start, so the stored fields come in order; bit 3 set again after the read (`seq::State::avg_raw_steps`) |
| `avg.debug_raw = wide` | the same with average mode kept: twice the points from the same start |

The raw read happens after the batch's own read and before the next arm, with the FSM stopped.

## Unit re-test

Build from `fix/b1-b3`. Signal: probe compensation 1 kHz square on CH1, edge trigger 0.15 V rising, as in the lab log.
Log: `/tmp/oos-ui.log`.

### B3

1. Acquire > Segments, HW segments on, Normal trigger, 100 ns/div. N = 10, 100, 1000 (`:ACQ:SEGM <n>;:ACQ:SEGM:HARD ON`).
   Expect: `segments: N of N recorded` after about N ms; the 0x4034 value in that line noted.
2. N = 1000 with the source at 100 Hz: the recording takes 10 s; STOP after about 3 s. Expect `k of 1000` with k near
   300, and k segments in the history.
3. Times (Segments list or `:ACQ:SEGM:TIMes?`): steps of 1.000 ms at 1 kHz (spread below 1 us), segment 0 at 0.
4. Log lines `segment k latch .. header ..` for k = 1..7: header(k) = latch(k - 1).
5. Interval: Seg. interval 5 ms, 1 kHz source, N = 20: steps of 5 or 6 ms (the next trigger after the interval).

### B1

Noise as in the lab log: 10 us/div, flat top, point-to-point difference / sqrt 2, three records each.

1. 62.5 MSa/s (10 us/div, depth 10k), HW average on. N = 2, 4, 16, 256. Expect roughly 1.1 mV / sqrt(N) down to the
   floor the software average reaches (SW 16: 0.31-0.41 mV). Note the log line `average Running batch k of c
   triggers ... words ...`: c is now the frames played per batch.
2. Settling: N = 16, step the input (probe off the comp terminal, then on). Count batches until the level settles:
   about 16 triggers for a block or running mean, more for an exponential one.
3. Fast rates with the old result expected (software average): 1 us/div (1.25 GSa/s): the top bar does not say "HW".
4. Fast rates in hardware: `avg.hw_fast = on` in /oos/system.conf, restart oos-ui. Same as step 1 at 1 us/div 1k
   (62.5 MSa/s, must still work), 1 us/div Auto (1.25 GSa/s), 10 us/div Auto (625 MSa/s), and at 125, 156 and 312 MSa/s
   (depths chosen for those rates). Record per rate: words zero or not. Zero only above 62.5 MSa/s: the divider path;
   zero only above 156 MSa/s: 0x1004 bit 2. Now that every frame is played, the fast rates may also work: then
   `avg_rate_ok` goes.
5. AvgSinc at fast rates: `avg.hw_fast = on` and `avg.debug_sinc = on`, restart oos-ui. The log shows
   `average debug: 0x1004 = 0x... (bit 2 forced)` after every configuration. 1 us/div Auto (1.25 GSa/s) and 10 us/div
   Auto (625 MSa/s): words zero or not. Not zero: bit 2 is the missing piece at fast rates, and the configuration sets
   it in average mode at every rate.
6. Both fields per point: `avg.debug_raw = seq` (and in a second run `= wide`), restart oos-ui, 62.5 MSa/s
   (10 us/div, 10k), N = 16, DC on CH1 (probe on the comp terminal's flat top, or a reference). The first 4 batches
   after every clear log `average raw Seq frame k: 20000 words, even: min .. max .. mean .. zero ../..; odd: ...` and
   save `/tmp/oos-avg-raw-<k>.bin` (u16 LE). Copy the files off. Read them as follows:
   - one parity near the signal level and the other 0 or constant: the play shows all there is, no hidden bits;
   - one parity the signal, the other varying in the low range (0..4095 on the stored scale, after the read path's
     affine mapping): candidate low bits. Check with N = 2 and N = 16 whether its spread grows with N;
   - `wide`: whether a longer window in average mode returns the second half of the point pairs or repeats.
   If the low bits are there, the decode joins the two fields per point; the format comes from these dumps.
7. Remove `avg.hw_fast`, `avg.debug_sinc` and `avg.debug_raw` afterwards.
